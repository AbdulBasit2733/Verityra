# Brand & Design System Directives

## Brand Voice & Identity
- **Tone**: Authoritative, academically rigorous, empathetic to competitive exam candidates, clean and modern.
- **Identity**: Verityra is an AI-powered evaluation platform for high-stakes examinations (UPSC, state civil services, senior secondary boards).

## Color System (60-30-10 Architecture)
- **Dominant 60%**: Neutral canvas and surfaces:
  - Light mode: Slate-50 (`#F8FAFC`) background, pure white (`#FFFFFF`) card containers.
  - Dark mode: Slate-950 canvas, Slate-900 elevated card containers.
- **Secondary 30%**: Structural and text elements:
  - Borders: Slate-200 (`#E2E8F0`) light, Slate-800 dark.
  - Headings: Slate-900 (`#0F172A`) light, Slate-100 dark.
  - Body text: Slate-600 (`#475569`) light, Slate-400 dark.
- **Accent 10% (Action Hero)**:
  - **Brand Primary**: Sapphire / Electric Indigo (`#2A6AED`).
  - Hover state: `#1E55C7`.
  - Use strictly for primary CTA buttons, active tab indicators, progress bar foregrounds, and key metric badges.

## Component Standards
- Buttons: Rounded-xl (`rounded-xl` / `rounded-2xl`), bold typography, crisp focus rings.
- Cards: `rounded-3xl`, soft border `border-slate-200/90`, subtle elevation shadows (`shadow-2xl shadow-blue-900/5`).
- Typography: Plus Jakarta Sans for headings (`font-heading font-black`), Inter/system sans for body.
