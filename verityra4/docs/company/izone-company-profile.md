## 3. Academic Scope — Courses & Exams Covered

I Zone Coaching Classes spans early school foundations through national entrance tests and language mastery:

### A. Pre-Primary, Primary & School Boards (Jr. KG – Class XII)
- **Early & Primary Foundation:** Jr. KG to Class V
- **Foundation Classes:** Class VI to Class X
- **Board Preparation:** **CBSE**, **ICSE**, and **Maharashtra State Board** (Class I to Class XII)

### B. Competitive & Entrance Examinations
- **Engineering Entrance:** **JEE (Main & Advanced)**
- **Medical Entrance:** **NEET-UG**
- **Scholarship & Aptitude:** **NTSE** and school-level **Olympiads**
- **Higher Education / Eligibility:** **UGC NET**

### C. Language & Grammar
- **English Grammar** (academic writing, syntax, comprehension, and composition)
- **Spoken English Training** (through **I Zone Training Academy**)

## 4. Explicit Domain Exclusions (Hard Rule)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> I Zone Coaching Classes **does NOT** coach or conduct preparation for government recruitment, civil services, or judicial examinations.
> **Strictly excluded from marketing, catalogs, defaults, and documentation:**
> - UPSC / Civil Services (IAS, IPS, IFS, GS Mains)
> - State PCS / MPSC / State Public Service Commission exams
> - Judicial Services / Judiciary & Law competitive exams
> - SSC CGL / Banking / Insurance / Defence recruitment exams
